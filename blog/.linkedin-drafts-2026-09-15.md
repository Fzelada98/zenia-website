## DRAFT score=15 — reactivar-clientes-inactivos-restaurante-whatsapp
WhatsApp reactivation flows for restaurants tend to fail for the same three reasons, and none of them are model quality.

We shipped a churn win-back agent last week on WhatsApp Business API. The interesting part isn't the LLM, it's the plumbing:

- POS pull (Agora / Revo / Toast) every 15 min into a CRM segmented by RFM
- Meta template pre-approval for cold outreach outside the 24h session window
- Opt-out interpreted as natural language, not keyword match, enforced at the message-queue layer

77.4% of first-time diners never return; the industry base erodes 30-40% every 90 days (Bloom Intelligence). Recovering 15-25% of that cohort per cycle turns a fixed-cost stack into a revenue channel.

Full write-up on the four-message sequence, the RFM cutoffs and the integration wiring: https://zeniapartners.com/blog/reactivar-clientes-inactivos-restaurante-whatsapp.html
