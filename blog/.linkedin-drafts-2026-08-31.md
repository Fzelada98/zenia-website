## DRAFT score=14 — agente-ia-para-clinicas-de-fisioterapia
A 3-therapist physio clinic in Spain loses 8 to 14 sessions a month to no-shows and half-finished packages.

At an average €42 ticket, that is €360 to €630 quietly walking out the door every week.

The default fix is "hire another receptionist." It rarely works, because the bottleneck is not headcount, it is what happens after 20:00 and on Sundays.

We put an AI agent on the clinic's existing WhatsApp number. It handles reschedules, tracks bono usage, flags patients who left mid-treatment, and hands the rest to a physio with the full context.

Six weeks in: no-shows dropped from 18% to 5%, full-package renewals climbed from 41% to 67%, reception got back 12 hours a week. That last one is the real prize.
