How it works

A full booking, without touching your phone

Watch a complete conversation: the agent answers, books, reminds and knows when to hand the chat to a person.

  1. The patient messages on WhatsAppAny time. Even at 11:40 pm, after you have closed.
  2. The agent answers with your informationTreatments, prices, hours and locations from your website, in your tone.
  3. It offers real slots from your calendarIt reads your calendar and only offers free times.
  4. It books and logs it in your CRMThe appointment lands in the calendar and the patient record, no typing.
  5. It sends a reminder the day beforeAnd if the patient can't make it, it reschedules in the same chat.
  6. Clinical questions go to your teamPain, emergencies or medical doubts: reception gets the whole conversation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp.

Does the agent replace my team?

No. It handles the repetitive questions, books and reminds. Anything that needs a person (complaints, special cases, emergencies) goes to your team with the whole conversation, and your team can step into any chat at any time.

Where does it get its information?

From your website, your prices and your rules. You review it before it goes live. What it does not know, it does not make up: it passes it to your team.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

We work with the official WhatsApp Business API. You can use your current number or a new one; on the call we explain the options and what each one involves.

What does it connect to?

WhatsApp Business API, Google Calendar, Calendly, HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Odoo, among others. If you use something else, we will look at it in the demo.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your conversation volume and what needs connecting. After showing you the demo with your business, we send you a fixed proposal with the monthly price.

How does the 48-hour demo work?

You leave us your website and we set up an agent with your public information. We send you a link to try it as if you were a customer, nothing to install.

Shall we look at it with your business?

We show you the demo with your information in 48 hours. No commitment.
