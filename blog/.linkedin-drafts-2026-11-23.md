## DRAFT score=16 — como-entrenar-un-agente-de-ia-para-mi-negocio
"Training an AI agent for your business" is almost never fine-tuning a model.

For 80-90% of SMB cases (RaftLabs, 2026), training an agent means four concrete artifacts: a versioned knowledge base (chunked, embedded, in a vector store), a system prompt with hard rules first and tone second, tool bindings to the CRM, WhatsApp Business API, calendar and payments, and a 50-100 conversation eval set that gates every prompt change.

Reported stack cost for that path: $5k-$30k to build, $200-$2k/month to run. Gartner's 2026 CIO survey (via Biz4Group): only 17% of organizations have shipped an AI agent in production.

The bottleneck is almost never model capability. It is the discipline of curating the knowledge base and keeping evals green week over week.

Reply if you are debugging agent drift in production, happy to compare notes on eval design.
