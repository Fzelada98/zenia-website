Case Study

A Viral Audience Is Not a Business. A Booking System Is.

An immigration advisory helping Latin Americans legally live, study and work in Spain had built a 23,000-follower social audience — and answered every inquiry by hand, on one phone, in one time zone. ZENIA built the acquisition system underneath: a conversion landing, a humanized AI agent that qualifies and books 24/7, a CRM with ad attribution, geo-targeted Google Ads and a layer of autonomous agents that optimize the whole loop every day.

Immigration advisory Spain ↔ LATAM AI Acquisition System
The Challenge

Demand Was Never the Problem. Capturing It Was.

The advisory's founder had done the hard part: built real trust with a large audience across Peru and the rest of Latin America, for a high-ticket service where each engagement is worth thousands of euros. But the operation behind the audience looked like most advisory firms:

  • Every inquiry handled manually on a personal phone — the same questions about requirements, prices and timelines answered dozens of times a week
  • A brutal time-zone gap: the audience is most active when Lima has dinner and Madrid sleeps. Messages sent at 2 a.m. Spain time waited hours for a reply — and cooled off
  • No qualification: nationals of countries the service legally cannot help queued next to perfect-fit clients
  • No CRM, no attribution: nobody could say which video, ad or post produced which consultation
  • No web presence to convert paid traffic — running ads would have meant sending clicks to a bio link

The service itself is complex and regulated: two distinct legal routes, milestone-based pricing, one hard financial requirement that stops most applicants and strict rules about which nationalities qualify. Explaining that correctly, one chat at a time, does not scale.

The Solution

A Full Acquisition System, Not a Widget

In under two weeks of build time, ZENIA designed and shipped the complete loop — from click to booked consultation — and then put autonomous agents in charge of running it:

  • Conversion landing with editorial identity: the service presented as a boarding pass from the client's country to Spain — two productized offers with transparent pricing and milestone-based payment, the hard requirement explained honestly instead of hidden, and legal pages ready for ad approval
  • A humanized AI agent, not a bot: the chat is staffed by a rotating three-person digital team covering morning, evening and overnight shifts. It answers in under a minute, in natural regional Spanish, qualifies by country and goal, explains both legal routes with the official requirement list, and pushes qualified leads toward the evaluation call
  • Built-in guardrails: the agent never invents requirements, prices or timelines, never promises outcomes, and politely redirects nationalities the firm legally cannot serve — before anyone wastes a call slot
  • Mini-CRM with attribution: every conversation lands in a live CRM with the campaign source (utm/gclid) attached, so cost-per-consultation is measurable per channel from day one
  • Geo-targeted Google Ads with a conversion pixel: search campaign aimed at the highest-intent city, capped budget and CPC, negative-keyword hygiene, sitelinks, and conversions fired the moment a chat starts
  • SEO content engine: twelve long-form guides answering the questions the audience actually asks, structured data, sitemap and Search Console wired in at launch — each guide carrying its own attribution tag into the CRM
  • Autonomous operations layer: a daily agent audits the ad account and the CRM, executes safe optimizations within hard budget caps and reports by message; a weekly agent reads real search-query data and publishes a new guide; a notifier pings the founder the moment a qualified lead or booking lands

The founder's role in acquisition is now one thing: show up to evaluation calls with people who already know the price, the requirements and the route that fits them.
